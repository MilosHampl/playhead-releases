Install

Download Playhead-macos.dmg, open it and drag Playhead into Applications. It needs macOS 12 or later and runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

To update, download the new DMG and replace the app in Applications. Your profiles, progress and settings are kept.

First launch

Test builds aren't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch:

  1. Open Playhead from Applications. When macOS says it can't verify it, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Playhead was blocked" and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

Right-click → Open no longer skips this check on macOS 15 and later. Alternative for Terminal users: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Playhead.app

Connect TMDB

Posters, cast, descriptions and the full movie and series catalogue come from The Movie Database. Each person uses their own free key:

  1. Create a free account at themoviedb.org.
  2. Open Settings → API and request a key (personal use).
  3. Copy either the API Key or the API Read Access Token.
  4. In Playhead, paste it in the first onboarding step or later in Settings → Catalog, and click Connect.

Without a key Playhead shows a small demo catalogue of free Blender open movies.

Sources

Playhead searches the sources listed in Settings → Sources and ranks what they return. The Internet Archive (public-domain films) is built in. You can add a Torznab indexer (for example your own Prowlarr or Jackett) or a Stremio add-on URL, and test each one from the same screen.

On a movie or episode page, Playhead picks the best release for your quality preset (Settings → Quality: Fast start, Balanced, Best quality), checks the top candidates' swarms live, and shows the pick under Play. Choose source lists every release with its score and the reasons some were filtered out.

Playing

  • Buffering shows its cause ("Finding peers…", "3.1 of 12 Mb/s needed"). If a source keeps stalling, Playhead counts down and switches to the next-best source at the same position.
  • Subtitles: embedded tracks are picked by your preferred language (Settings → Subtitles). Drag an .srt, .ass or .vtt file onto the player to add one. Size, colour, background and position are in the player's settings.
  • Right-click the video for the menu: audio and subtitle tracks, speed, aspect and zoom, chapters, sleep timer, mini player, screenshot.
  • Continue watching: progress is saved while you watch; a title counts as watched at 90 %.

Profiles and sync

Each profile has its own watch history, My List and playback preferences; switch at the bottom of the sidebar. Guest lets someone watch without keeping anything.

Settings → Profiles & data exports everything to one file and imports it back (API keys only if you choose to include them). Sync keeps several Macs in step through a folder you already sync — iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Syncthing; the newest change wins.

Disk and network

  • Cache: Playhead keeps at most the cache limit on disk while playing (1 GB by default, Settings → Cache), keeps a stopped title for 5 minutes so you can resume instantly, then removes it. Settings → Cache → Clear cache removes it now.
  • Uploading: like every BitTorrent client, Playhead shares pieces with other peers while it plays, capped at 1 MB/s by default (Settings → Network). Other peers can see your IP address.
  • What leaves your Mac: TMDB requests (with your key), searches to your sources, BitTorrent traffic, and OpenSubtitles requests if you add a key. No analytics or tracking.

Troubleshooting

"Playhead can't be opened" / "damaged"

Follow First launch. If macOS still refuses, run the xattr command there.

"No sources found"

Check Settings → Sources: at least one source must be enabled and pass its Test. Public-domain titles are found through the Internet Archive; other titles need a source that carries them.

It keeps buffering

The overlay says why. A slow or dead swarm is switched automatically after a short countdown; you can also pick another release under Choose source.

Start over

Quit Playhead and delete ~/Library/Containers/com.miloshampl.playhead — this removes all profiles, progress and settings (export first if you want to keep them).

Reporting a problem

Open an issue with the version (Settings → About), your macOS version and Mac model, what you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
