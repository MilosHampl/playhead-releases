Play while it downloads.

Playhead streams a torrent's video in playback order through a small, bounded cache and plays it in a native macOS player. When a source stalls it switches to the next one on its own.

Test build · macOS 12 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

First launch. This test build isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time:
  1. Open the DMG and drag Playhead into Applications, then open it. macOS says it can't verify it — click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Playhead was blocked" and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only do this once.

What it does

Starts in about a second

Downloads the pieces just ahead of the playhead, so a good source starts playing almost at once, and seeks land in a few seconds.

Bounded disk use

Keeps a fixed-size cache (1 GB by default) no matter how big the file is, and removes it when you stop.

Picks a healthy source

Ranks releases by quality and checks their swarms live before playing; if one dies mid-film it switches to the next at the same moment.

A proper player

Hardware decoding, subtitles (SRT, ASS, PGS) with styling, audio and subtitle delay, chapters, mini player, keyboard shortcuts.

Rich details

Cast, crew, facts, trailers and collections from The Movie Database, with your own free API key.

Profiles

Separate watch history and lists per person, a guest mode, export to a file and sync between Macs through a folder.
